lattes, juice, soda, bubble tea, protein shakes, beer, and any other drink

is_food true and estimate from the text:

(Meituan): braised chicken rice, soy milk" or "Packaged soy milk, 250 mL
(from nutrition label)".

DO NOT invent a meal from an image that only SHOWS food without evidence it
was eaten by this user. These are is_food false:

  - an empty cart, a cancelled order, or a wishlist.
A phantom meal is worse than a missing one: when the evidence of an actual
order or of consumption is not clear, answer {"is_food": false}.
